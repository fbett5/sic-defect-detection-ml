Reliable: defects that add or remove material where the reference has none (short, spurious copper, pin-hole; on SEM a particle, bridge or open), and the plain question "is there a defect here?" (see AP50 any-defect).

Less reliable: look-alike edge defects (open vs mousebite, spur vs copper), the smallest size tertile, and exact box extent (compare mAP50 with mAP50-95). Without a reference image, expect more of both false alarms and misses (see the gray ablation).

Not detectable by design: defect types that are not in training (they get a low score or the nearest known class, which is why nothing is auto-closed), and failures with no visible signature.

Data caveats: DeepPCB is binarised PCB imagery, some defects are synthetic, references are near-perfectly aligned, and the clean controls are templates. Absolute numbers will not transfer to SEM; the method will. Expect registration error to be the main false-alarm source on real die-to-die images.
